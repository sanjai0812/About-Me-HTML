# About-Me-HTML

Personal "About Me" Page:

Concept: A single-page website that introduces yourself.
HTML Elements: <h1> (heading), <p> (paragraph), <img> (image), <ul> and <li> (unordered list), <a> (link).
Features:
A title with your name.
A short bio.
A picture of yourself.
A list of your hobbies or interests.
Links to your social media profiles.
